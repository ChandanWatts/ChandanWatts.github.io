Research Focus

Environmental Drivers

My research focuses on galaxy formation and evolution, especially the role of environment in shaping galaxy properties. I study galaxies across different environments, from individual galaxies and groups to dense clusters, to understand how processes such as galaxy interactions and mergers influence their evolution.

Internal Mechanisms & Morphology

Using integral-field spectroscopy (MUSE, MaNGA) and multiwavelength data (UV, optical, IR) to explore internal evolutionary drivers. This includes tracing star formation, AGN activity, galactic outflows, and the dynamics of barred galaxies.

Peculiar Case Studies

Detailed observational studies of post-interaction/merger galaxies. Current highlights include mapping the intermediate stage of polar-ring formation in NGC 3718 and analyzing an extended and longest tidal tail (~380 kpc) in NGC 3785 that hosts potential ultra-diffuse galaxy (UDG) formation.
